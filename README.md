📌 Project Overview
An interactive Power BI dashboard analysing European retail sales data across 16+ countries. The dashboard provides real-time insights into quantity sold, revenue distribution, and geographic market penetration — helping businesses make faster, smarter decisions.
📈 Key Metrics
Metric	Value	Insight
Total Revenue (Unit Price Sum)	£2,498,803.97	Full period sales value
Total Quantity Sold	5,176,450 units	High volume operations
UK Market Share	92.71%	Primary market dependency
International Share	7.29%	Growth opportunity
Countries Covered	16+	Pan-European reach
🗺️ Dashboard Features
Interactive map showing quantity & unit price by country across Europe
Bar chart comparing Sum of Quantity vs Sum of UnitPrice by country
Donut chart showing CustomerID count vs Quantity split (92.71% / 7.29%)
KPI cards for total UnitPrice sum and total Quantity at a glance
💡 Business Impact
This dashboard enables non-technical business users to instantly understand where sales are coming from, which markets are underperforming, and where the biggest growth opportunities lie — without needing to read a single spreadsheet.
Who benefits from this dashboard?
Sales managers — identify top-performing and underperforming countries
Finance teams — track revenue and plan quarterly forecasts
Operations teams — align inventory levels with demand by region
Executive leadership — one-page view of entire European operations
